MMC bonus tolerance

Adds the size departure from MMC to a geometric tolerance applied at MMC.

Internal bonus = actual size − MMC

Fixed fastener budget

Splits the available position budget between a clearance hole and a fixed/threaded hole whose axis is orientation-controlled.

Tclear + Tthread = H − F

Projected-zone check

Screens the diametrical drift caused by axis angle over a projected height.

Worst case = base position + 2P × tan(θ)

Composite vs. stacked

Composite

One characteristic symbol spans multiple segments. The upper segment locates the pattern; lower segments refine feature-to-feature relationships.

Stacked

Each row is a complete, independent control with its own characteristic, tolerance, and datum references.
